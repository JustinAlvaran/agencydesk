# AgencyDesk product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users and purpose

Agency staff capture inquiries, manage contacts and follow-ups, publish versioned proposals, and deliver projects. Clients review their company's proposals, milestones, and shared conversations. Recruiters explore a read-only fictional demonstration of the same workflow.

## Constraints confirmed by the user

Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind, Supabase, and Vercel. Preserve the inquiry-to-delivery workflow, live permissions, immutable published pricing/scope, and transactional proposal acceptance. Reference projects supply practices only, never content or data.

The user prioritizes straightforward UI/UX over complex or decorative design, rejects the blue palette, and explicitly prohibits substituting a text wordmark for a logo. No approved logo exists. Use ordinary navigation and screen titles; do not invent branding.

## Evidence

All agency/sample records are fictional. Existing browser, unit, and database tests cover the business workflow and permissions. No testimonials or commercial results are supplied.

## Principles

Make the next action easy to find. Use literal labels. Make records easy to scan and inspect. Keep demo restrictions clear. Preserve keyboard and mobile access.
