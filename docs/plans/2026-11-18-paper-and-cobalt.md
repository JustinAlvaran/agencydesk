# Paper and cobalt

Give visitors a clear route from services to inquiry and give staff a compact workspace for daily lead review. Use warm paper, ink, and a single cobalt action color throughout marketing, authentication, the CRM, the client portal, and the A2UI demo. Keep workflow statuses distinguishable in text.

The design draws on Bag UI's free Hero 4, Navbar 2, and Sidebar 1 at revision [7e52bb4](https://github.com/anelkabag/bag-ui/tree/7e52bb4cdf17877f7108a27d8e6d6ea32bacfb0d/registry/default). Adapt the rounded navigation container, split hero, and grouped rail to the existing components. Keep AgencyDesk's routes, accessible mobile dialog, interactive world, and read-only demonstration boundaries.

Verify the inquiry link, mobile navigation, lead details, keyboard focus, and desktop/mobile fit. Capture public and fictional demo screens. Roll back by reverting the presentation changes through preview. No migrations, paid components, or new runtime integrations are required.
