## Art direction

Use a rich forest-green hero, crisp white typography, generous spacing and a clean white form area. Introduce a restrained warm coral accent for primary actions, inspired by the energy of HubSpot. Keep the application itself bright and readable, with white surfaces, subtle gray borders and compact navigation.

The result should look like a mature CRM product. Avoid generic beige editorial templates, oversized italic slogans, gradient blobs, sparkles, decorative blue markings, excessive rounded cards and meaningless badges. Design for useful work, with excellent hierarchy, alignment, typography and consistent controls.

## Create our brand

Design an original AgencyDesk wordmark and simple geometric symbol that communicates organized relationships and connected work. Produce reusable SVG versions for light backgrounds, dark backgrounds, the application sidebar and favicon. Do not imitate Zoho's or HubSpot's marks. Give AgencyBot a complementary original avatar.

## Sign-up and login

The Bigin reference is a sign-up layout. On desktop, place a strong product headline and concise benefits on the left, with an immediately visible account form on the right. The account form has full name, email and password. Include visible labels, a password visibility toggle, password guidance, accessible validation and a clear action. Include an existing-account sign-in link.

AgencyDesk is for OUR agency's staff and invited clients, not public self-service agencies. Treat this sign-up design as invitation completion. For a verified invitation, show the invited account's email read-only, allow display-name/password setup and preserve the administrator-assigned access. Without an invitation, show an honest request-access path. Do not auto-create a workspace, grant staff access, or simulate registration success.

Create a separate sign-in screen with email, password and Forgot password. Include invitation-expired, recovery, loading and error states. On mobile keep the introduction short and bring the form forward. Do not invent free-trial offers, data-residency promises or legal links; use approved destinations only.

## Homepage

Borrow HubSpot's clarity: a concise product promise, two clear actions and a large realistic CRM preview. Explain the workflow: Find prospects → qualify leads → manage conversations → send proposals → deliver projects. Show concrete capabilities and recognizable product screens. Label fictional demo records. Do not invent customers, testimonials, awards or business results.

## CRM experience

Build a cohesive workspace with:

- Overview prioritizing follow-ups, pending decisions and project delivery.
- Discover leads with search criteria, source-backed prospects, fit explanations, missing-information and duplicate indicators, and review before import. Unknown contact details stay unknown. Keep prospects separate from qualified CRM leads.
- Lead pipeline with searchable table/board, clear stages, ownership and next action.
- Lead details with contact information, source, notes, activity, tasks and linked proposals.
- Proposals, projects, contacts and a simpler client portal restricted to that client's shared records.
- A Connections concept for Gmail, WhatsApp and Instagram. Actual integrations are deferred. Clearly label unconfigured states; never simulate a successful connection.

## AgencyBot

Add a polished assistant called AgencyBot, inspired by the HubSpot screenshot: compact launcher, branded header, minimize/close controls, readable messages, input box and useful suggestions.

Suggestions: Find potential leads, Review my pipeline, Draft a follow-up, Help with connections.

Visitors receive public product help. Signed-in staff receive only authorized CRM information. Show sources for research and mark proposed changes for human review. Never automatically import prospects, send messages, approve proposals or grant permissions. Never claim an action succeeded without its actual result. In a frontend prototype, explicitly label responses as demonstrations. Keep the launcher clear of forms and primary actions, with a suitable mobile drawer and keyboard focus management.
